ABOUT RESEARCHMIND AI

Research smarter. Understand deeper.

ResearchMind AI is a multi-agent research and intelligence platform designed to transform complex questions into structured, evidence-based reports.

THE PLATFORM

What is ResearchMind AI?

ResearchMind AI is a research system where specialized AI agents collaborate to complete a research task.

The platform divides work between agents responsible for planning, web research, fact checking, report generation, and final review.

The goal is to make research more organized and easier to inspect by presenting findings alongside available sources.
